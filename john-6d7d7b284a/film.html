The story

A monk, a rock star, and a maximum-security prisoner.

It doesn’t matter if you are a monk, a rock star or a maximum-security prisoner. While we all have different realities, we are all searching for connection and joy.

Through music, lived experience, and neuroscience, EMPATHY explores what helps us move through pain, to self-regulation, and back into connection with ourselves and each other.

Ancient wisdom. A contemporary, scientific lens.
